About

Understanding Fourier Series

Core concepts behind the visualization platform.

📐

Fourier Series

A mathematical tool that decomposes any periodic function into a sum of sine and cosine terms. It's fundamental to signal processing, acoustics, and quantum mechanics.

🎵

Harmonic Components

Each harmonic is a sine or cosine wave with a frequency that is an integer multiple of the fundamental frequency. Together they reconstruct the original waveform.

📈

Convergence

As more harmonics are added, the Fourier series approximation converges to the original waveform. This is the core principle behind the animation.

🎯

Project Objective

To provide an interactive, intuitive, and visually stunning platform for understanding Fourier series convergence, suitable for students and researchers alike.
